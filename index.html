Our Mission

Democratizing neuroscience research through open-source AI

Neuropharmacology research generates hundreds of thousands of papers annually. Drug interactions, receptor binding profiles, and mechanism pathways are scattered across siloed databases and paywalled journals.

NeuroLens AI processes this literature locally — no cloud dependency, no subscription fees, full data privacy — and publishes curated findings openly for researchers, students, and the public.

Platform

What we're building

Three core tools powered by local LLM inference on NVIDIA hardware

🧠

Literature Analysis Engine

Feed thousands of neuropharmacology papers into locally-running LLMs to extract drug interactions, receptor binding affinities, and mechanism-of-action pathways. All processed on-device with zero data leaving your machine.

⚗

Drug Interaction Query

Ask natural-language questions about neurotransmitter-drug relationships and get cited answers drawn from analyzed literature. Like having a pharmacology expert available 24/7 on your local workstation.

🌐

Open Research Portal

We publish our curated findings — drug interaction maps, receptor profiles, and literature syntheses — freely to the public. Neuroscience knowledge shouldn't be locked behind paywalls.

About

Building at the intersection of neuroscience and AI

I'm an engineering student at Citrus College in Azusa, CA, pursuing transfer to a CSU/UC program with a focus on neuropharmacology and biomedical engineering.

NeuroLens AI started from a simple observation: the tools to analyze neuroscience literature at scale are either prohibitively expensive or locked in the cloud. I'm building an alternative — affordable, local, open-source, and powered by NVIDIA GPUs.

My goal is to make neuropharmacology research accessible to independent researchers, students, and anyone curious about how drugs interact with the brain.
